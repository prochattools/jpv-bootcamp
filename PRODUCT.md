# Product

## Users

JPV Bootcamp serves aspiring and experienced property investors who want practical education, live guidance, a faith-driven community, and a clear route into their member account. Visitors compare the monthly and annual membership; members sign in to learn, manage billing, use resources, and participate in available community features; administrators operate the platform through a separate protected back office.

## Product Purpose

The platform brings JPV Bootcamp marketing, membership purchase, account security, learning, community, support, billing, and administration into one coherent application. Success means a visitor can understand and purchase the membership, a member can securely complete every account and learning journey, an administrator can operate the platform, and every communication looks and sounds recognizably JPV Bootcamp.

## Brand Personality

Purposeful, hopeful, and grounded. JPV Bootcamp should feel bright and human without becoming playful or casual about property, money, faith, security, or account access. The public site builds confidence; product and transactional surfaces reduce uncertainty and help people complete the next step.

## Anti-references

- Generic dark SaaS styling, neon glows, blue-purple gradients, and decorative glass.
- Corporate property-investment clichés, artificial luxury, invented wealth claims, or pressure tactics.
- Kairos branding, photography, testimonials, artwork, proprietary copy, or code copied literally.
- Disconnected public, login, member, email, and administrator experiences.
- Decorative complexity that competes with checkout, account access, learning, or support.

## Design Principles

1. One recognizable JPV system across public pages, account screens, member surfaces, administrator customizations, notifications, and email.
2. Purpose before decoration: every visual choice must make the next action or the current state easier to understand.
3. Honest evidence: never invent testimonials, outcomes, capabilities, teachers' biographies, or operational claims.
4. Warm confidence: generous space, bright brand color, clear hierarchy, and calm language without artificial urgency.
5. Preserve working boundaries: design changes must not alter authorization, billing, provider, migration, or delivery behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks and headings, associated form labels, useful status announcements, 44px touch targets where practical, reduced-motion support, color-independent state cues, readable line lengths, and contrast checks for text and controls. Responsive behavior must work without horizontal overflow at narrow mobile, tablet, and wide desktop sizes.
